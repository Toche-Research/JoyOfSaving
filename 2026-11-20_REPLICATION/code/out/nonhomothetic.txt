  k_MGR=8.5052  k_GR=17.2133  k_max=89.4427

0. Check: with the paper's utility theta* is strictly increasing on (k_MGR, k_max) and its root
   equals the steady state of the paper's own solver at theta = 0.05, 0.2, 1 (to 1e-9).

1. Necessary condition (any concave u, v): sigma_c(c(k)) > Gamma(k) for some k in (k_MGR, k_GR).
   min Gamma = 25.878, at k = 1.504 k_MGR, where tau = 0.238
   => the EIS 1/sigma_c must fall below 0.0386 at some steady-state consumption level
   largest elasticity of steady-state consumption in capital on (k_MGR,k_GR): eps_c(k_MGR) = 0.1579
